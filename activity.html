About

The data comes from the KS server, which reads Torn's public faction list every 30 seconds. Only time when the server actually saw the faction is counted, so a gap in its readings is never shown as activity or as quiet. All times are TCT (UTC). A square is the share of that time of day, across the days covered, that the member was Online; the colour goes from dark (never) to light green (almost always). The strip above the members shows when the faction is most active, compared with its own busiest time in the window; Quietest/Busiest and its hover line give the number of members online. This page is information; it never changes who may take a DIBS. Its only call to Torn is the list of names, made with your key, at most once every 10 minutes per faction. Nothing is saved in this browser except the same key War Room uses.
