# kingshade-leader-tools

## Userscripts

- `KS_Black_Market_PC.user.js` — Black Market travel advisor for PC: best runs abroad and when to leave Torn to land after a restock.
- `KS_Black_Market_PDA.user.js` — Black Market travel advisor for Torn PDA: same advice as the PC version.
- `KS_Chain_Dibs_PC.user.js` — Chain Dibs for PC: floating window with the chain timer, hit queue and "I'm here" rule.
- `KS_Chain_Dibs_PDA.user.js` — Chain Dibs for Torn PDA: same chain queue, minimized to a Dibs row under Torn's status bars.
- `KS_OC_Planner_PC.user.js` — OC Planner for PC: shows role advice and suggested line-ups on Faction > Crimes.
- `KS_Racing_Lab_PC.user.js` — Racing Lab for PC: shows the value of each racing part for your chosen car and track.
- `KS_Torn_Hustling_Advisor_PC.user.js` — Read-only Hustling advisor for PC: shows one recommended manual action at a time.
- `KS_Torn_War_Dibs_PC.user.js` — War Dibs for PC: DIBS as a native roster column with FF and Est from FFScouter.
- `KS_Torn_War_Dibs_PDA.user.js` — War Dibs for Torn PDA: roster-local DIBS presentation with shared-claim safety.
- `KS_War_Tools_Torn_PDA.user.js` — Retired War Tools script for Torn PDA; does nothing and only silences old installs (merged into Kingshade Scout).
- `Kingshade_Scout_Torn_PDA.user.js` — Kingshade Scout for Torn PDA: FF/Est, live faction status, countdowns, filters and sorting.
- `Kingshades_Bootlegging_Advisor.user.js` — Bootlegging advisor for Torn PDA with queue balancing and privacy-safe diagnostics.
