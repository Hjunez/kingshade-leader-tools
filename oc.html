What this needs, and what the numbers mean
Key
faction → crimes and faction → members, plus faction API access on your position.
CPR
The pass rate Torn reports for that seat. On a filled seat it is the member's rate. On an empty seat it is your own rate for that role — so an empty seat tells you whether you should take it.
The minimum CPR line
Your faction's own admission rule: level 1 needs 50%, level 2 needs 60%, levels 3 and 4 need 65%. Anything above level 4 is held to the same 65% until you say otherwise. Each crime shows the line it has to clear, and seats under it are marked. This is a faction decision, not a Torn mechanic.
Item
Seats that need an item say so, and whether the person holding the seat actually has it.
