First time here?

Ask KS Leslie needs nothing — just open it. The other three read your faction through Torn's API. Make one Custom Key in Torn under Settings → API Keys and tick these five boxes under faction:

members · rankedwars · rankedwarreport · crimes · news

Ticking the boxes is only half of it. Your faction position also needs Faction API Access, which the Leader sets on the position in Torn, not on the key. Without it, crimes, the armory log and the armory contents all come back refused even though every box is ticked and you can see the same data in Torn.

Sign in above and the cards below will tell you which tools your key actually opens, and why any of them are closed.

Your key stays in your own browser. Every tool here sends it only to Torn, with one exception: Faction Events signs you in to Kingshade's own event server so the board knows you are in the faction. That server asks Torn who the key belongs to and then throws it away — it never stores it.

These pages read through Torn's official API and never act on your behalf in Torn. Faction Events is the one thing here that stores anything: the events live on Kingshade's own server, and only the Leader and Co-Leader can put them there.

The status beside each name is that person's status in Torn, read once when this page opens. It is not a signal that they have this page open — nothing here watches anyone.
