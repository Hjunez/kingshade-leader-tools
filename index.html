These pages read through Torn's official API and never act on your behalf in Torn. Your key is saved in your own browser. Every tool here sends it only to Torn, with one exception: Faction Events sends it to Kingshade's own event server to sign you in. That server asks Torn who the key belongs to and then throws it away — it never stores it. Faction Events also asks Torn for the faction's member names once when you sign in and when you press Refresh, to show who answered an event or is away. Your answers, absences and messages to the staff are stored on Kingshade's own server; reasons and messages can be read only by the staff (Rabid, Cheese, Kingshade, MooSlayerOfGods, Tatz and Kneato).
