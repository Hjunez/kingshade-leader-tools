First time here?

Ask KS Leslie needs nothing — just open it. The other three read your faction through Torn's API. Make one Custom Key in Torn under Settings → API Keys and tick these five boxes under faction:

members · rankedwars · rankedwarreport · inventory · news

That one key covers every tool here. Nothing outside faction is needed, and nothing is ever written — these pages only read.

Your key stays in your own browser and is only ever sent to Torn. Miss one of the five and you get error 16, which is a tick box, not a broken key.

These pages read through Torn's official API and never act on your behalf. No account, no login, nothing stored on any server.

The status beside each name is that person's status in Torn, read once when this page opens. It is not a signal that they have this page open — nothing here watches anyone.
