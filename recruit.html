How this page uses your key
Purpose
Find active players without a faction for recruitment. It reads only when you press Sign in, Save key or Find recruits. Nothing runs in the background.
Calls
api.torn.com only: key/info, user/snapshot twice (today and 7 days ago), and user/{id}/personalstats for up to the number of players you choose to check in detail. At most 50 calls a minute, one at a time. Every call is tagged KSRecruitFinder in your own API log. The key goes in a request header, never in a web address.
Storage
Your browser only. The key stays until you remove it (under ks-leader-board-key, shared with the other Leader Tools pages, or ks-recruit-finder-key). The result list and your filters are kept under ks-recruit-finder-v1 until the next search. Torn's player snapshot itself is never saved.
Sharing
Nobody. There is no server and no account. The key is not in the saved list or the copied text.
Key access
Custom: user → snapshot, personalstats. The key must belong to a current member of faction 48039.
Writes
None. This page only reads. It never opens a Torn page, clicks or sends anything in Torn; profile links open only when you click them.

How to read this list
Who is listed
Players in Torn's daily snapshot of active players with no faction, not in federal jail, and inside the filters above. Click a heading to sort.
Hours/day (7 d)
Time played in today's snapshot minus time played in the snapshot from 7 days ago, divided by 7. A player missing from the older snapshot shows a dash and sorts last.
Donator
As reported in Torn's daily player snapshot.
Account age
Whole days since the player signed up.
Streak to RW wins
From Torn's personal stats, read only for the players with the most hours per day, up to the number you choose to check in detail. The rest show a dash.
Dash
Torn did not give the number, or it was not read. A dash is never a zero; hover it for the reason.
