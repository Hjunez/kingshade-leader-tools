What this needs, and what it can and cannot tell you
Key
faction → rankedwars and faction → rankedwarreport. Both are public-level calls, so this works even without faction API access on your position.
Blank cells
A blank means the member does not appear in that war's report. That can mean they made no hits, or that they were not in the faction yet. Torn does not say which, so the page does not guess.
Former members
Names in the report who are no longer on the roster are shown greyed out, so old numbers do not make your current roster look better or worse than it is.
Respect
Switch the table to respect if you split payouts that way. Same data, same wars.

Read this as attendance, not as worth. A member with few hits may have been asleep in another timezone, hospitalised, or new. The table tells you who to talk to, not who to remove.
