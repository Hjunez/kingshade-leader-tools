First time here?

These tools read your faction through Torn's API. Make one Custom Key in Torn under Settings → API Keys and tick these boxes:

Under faction: members · rankedwars · rankedwarreport · crimes · news · inventory · attacks

Under user: personalstats · snapshot · profile (Recruit Finder needs personalstats and snapshot, and profile to find players who joined a faction since Torn's snapshot)

Create the Leader Tools key

Ticking the boxes is only half of it. Your faction position also needs Faction API Access, which the Leader sets on the position in Torn, not on the key. Without it, crimes, the armory log and the armory contents all come back refused even though every box is ticked and you can see the same data in Torn.

Sign in above and the cards below will tell you which tools your key actually opens, and why any of them are closed.

Your key stays in your own browser. Every tool here sends it only to Torn, with one exception: Faction Events signs you in to Kingshade's own event server so the board knows you are in the faction. That server asks Torn who the key belongs to and then throws it away — it never stores it.

Ready to use

Live Member Activity Board Who is still here, who has gone quiet, and who is in hospital, jail or abroad right now. Quiet members are flagged and the list copies out for messaging. Open Live War Payout Ledger Pulls every member's hits straight from the war report, splits the cache money by hits or respect, and copies a clean summary into faction chat. Open Live Armory & Loaner Audit Who is holding faction property right now, what is left in the armory, and the recent take-and-return log. Copies the chase list. Open Live OC Roster Board Every crime in progress, who sits where, which seats are open with your own pass rate for them, and who is free to fill one. Open Beta KS OC Planner The whole OC plan for the leaders: who sits where, who should move, who is free and who is holding a crime up. Member status is read live from Torn. Open Live Key Check Tests your key against every call these tools make, and says whether a failure is a missing tick box you can fix or a faction permission only the Leader can. Open Live War Participation History Hits per member across your recent wars, in one table. Turns who actually shows up from an argument into a number. Open Ended Halloween 2026 results Final standings from the Halloween event, 25 Oct – 1 Nov 2026, for every member. Attacks, respect, records, activity and treats, with each member's rank and a side-by-side comparison. Open Beta Recruit Finder Active players without a faction who meet our bar: donator, level range and real daily play time from Torn's own player snapshot. It tracks the players you have already contacted and those who have since joined a faction, and shows Elo and, with your own FFScouter key, estimated battle stats. One click, a ranked list with profile links. Open Beta Faction Recruit Intelligence An applicant laid out in full — level against the faction's own spread, what the applicant shared and what they did not, and every time they applied before. No score, no verdict; the call stays yours. Runs on example data for now, not live applications. Open

These pages read through Torn's official API and never act on your behalf in Torn. Faction Events is the one thing here that stores anything: the events, the answers to them, absences and messages to the staff live on Kingshade's own server. Only the Leader and Co-Leader can put events there, and only the staff (Rabid, Cheese, Kingshade, MooSlayerOfGods, Tatz and Kneato) can read reasons and messages.

The status beside each name is that person's status in Torn, read once when this page opens. It is not a signal that they have this page open — nothing here watches anyone.
