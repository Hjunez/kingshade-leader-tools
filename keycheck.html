Key Check

Tries your key against every call these tools make and tells you exactly what works, what does not, and which of the two things is wrong: the key, or your faction position.

Checks

Seven calls, one per thing the tools need.

Two different problems look identical from the outside. A missing tick box on the key gives error 16. A faction position without permission gives error 7. Only the second needs your Leader — this page tells you which one you have.

Nothing is written and nothing is stored on a server. The key is tested and kept in this browser only.
