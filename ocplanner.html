What this needs, and what happens to the key
Key

Sign-in
The key goes once to the KS OC Planner server, which only asks Torn whose key it is and checks that you are on the OC leader list. The server never stores the key. The sign-in slip it hands back lives in this page only and expires after about ten minutes.
Torn
The page asks Torn directly, from this browser, for the faction member list, to see who is in hospital, in jail or abroad. The key goes to Torn only, in the request header.
Presses
One press of Load or Refresh is one read. The page never polls in the background and never acts inside Torn.
