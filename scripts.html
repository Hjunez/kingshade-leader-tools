Every Kingshade userscript in one place. Pick the one for the device you play on, install it, and you are done.

Userscripts
Before you install

On PC: you need Tampermonkey in your browser first. Install it from the Chrome Web Store or the Firefox add-ons site, then come back here and press Install.

On Torn PDA: the app has its own script list. Open Torn PDA, then Settings → Advanced browser settings → Manage scripts → + → Configure (next to Remote load/update) → Remote URL → paste the address shown on the card (press Copy) → Fetch → Load → Continue. If an older copy with the same name is already there, delete it first: Torn PDA will not add a second script with the same name, but still says it was added.

On PC, if pressing Install just opens a page full of text, nothing is broken — select all of it, copy it, and paste it into a new script in Tampermonkey.

Ranked war coming up? Read the Ranked War DIBS guide — how to update, how to check your version, and how DIBS works.

For Torn PC

PC KS Torn War Dibs Version 2.0.0 Shared DIBS for ranked war, drawn straight into Torn's own Attack column. One target, one caller, no double hits. The PC and Torn PDA versions share the same DIBS through the faction's own KS War Room server.
Runs onTorn faction pages
Networkapi.torn.com, ffscouter.com (FF and Est only) and the KS War Room server warroom.kingshadeprotocol.com (DIBS, and Share with KS if you turn it on). Nothing else.
StoresYour keys (encrypted) and settings in your own browser. Your Torn key is sent to the KS War Room server only to sign you in; the server asks Torn who you are and does not store the key. The server keeps the claims (target, your Torn ID, times) and deletes them when the war has been untouched for 12 hours. Share with KS is off by default; when you turn it on, your own battle stats (strength, defense, speed, dexterity, total) and your Torn ID go to the KS War Room server every 6 hours while your own war page is open, are used only for the faction's stat estimates and are never shown to anyone.
Torn keyYes. Faction members and wars, user basic and profile, and user battlestats (used only by Share with KS).

PC KS Black Market Version 0.4.5 Your travel advisor: the most profitable runs abroad for every item, and when to leave Torn to land right after a restock — with the chance the stock is still there. For members of PoIitically Incorrect only.
Runs onAny Torn page (a KS Black Market box in Torn's left sidebar, or a small BM button bottom left)
NetworkOnly the KS Black Market server blackmarket.kingshadeprotocol.com. Nothing else — it never contacts Torn itself.
ReadsThe cash shown in Torn's sidebar, and the Travel Agency page when you open it (travel method and item slots) — only while the tab is visible. Nothing it reads is sent anywhere. It also reads the colours and font of Torn's page so the panel looks like Torn.
StoresYour Torn key, travel method, item slots, sort choice and starred or hidden items in your script manager. Your key is sent to the KS Black Market server only to sign you in; the server asks Torn which faction you are in and does not store the key. The sign-in (valid 10 minutes) and the latest prices are kept in your script manager so the box works on every page.
Torn keyYes. A Public key is enough. Only used to check that you are in PoIitically Incorrect.

PC KS Chain Dibs Version 0.8.0 A small chain window on every Torn page: the chain timer, a queue for the next hit and the Im here rule, so the chain does not break. Only the member who made the hit leaves the queue. When anybody may hit, FREE HIT links to a free target, and if the hitter fails his attack one button lets everybody hit at once. For members of PoIitically Incorrect only.
Runs onAny Torn page (a small window, top left)
Networkapi.torn.com and the KS War Room server warroom.kingshadeprotocol.com. Nothing else, and no requests to torn.com pages.
ReadsThe chain bar in Torn's side menu (hits and time left), read only, on the page you have open.
StoresYour Torn key in Tampermonkey on this PC. It is sent to the KS War Room server once per sign-in to confirm your Torn ID and faction; the server never stores it. The server keeps the chain queue (Torn IDs, join time, Im here, and how many hits in a row you stood first without pressing it), who released the current hit and why, and the latest chain reading, deleted 12 hours after the last change, for one minute the Torn IDs of members it removed as AFK, and the free hit list set by the faction leader. Your own attacks, energy and status are read only to see if you made the chain hit or can hit; only the fact that you made the hit is sent to the server.
Torn keyYes. A custom key with user basic, attacks and bars, and faction chain and members. The window has a link that creates exactly that key.

These scripts read Torn's official API and the page you have open yourself. None of them click, attack, travel or act for you — every move in Torn stays yours.

War Dibs keeps your Torn key encrypted in your own browser and also sends it to the faction's KS War Room server to sign you in; that server does not store it. KS Scout keeps its key unencrypted in your own browser or Torn PDA and sends it only to Torn and FFScouter, and to the KS War Room server to sign you in if you turn Share with KS on.
